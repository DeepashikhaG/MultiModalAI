All employees who travel on Company business will be covered under this policy. Trainees and consultants will also be covered. The employees are to use the company’s preferred hotels for their stay while on business trips and ensure that the appropriate corporate discounts are extended to them while settling the bills. For convenience, and better planning the Administration department should be consulted. The following rates are applicable to the employees. Any deviation needs to have a prior approval from the Director/CEO.
